# Icon notices

The starter depends on 211 icon sets of [Iconify](https://iconify.design), one `@iconify-json/<prefix>` package each, under the license of each set. The icons are rendered offline: nothing is downloaded at runtime. Generated by `pnpm update:icon-sets`; do not edit by hand.
